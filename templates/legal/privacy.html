What Miller stores

Your account. When you sign in with Google: your email address, name and profile picture. Before you sign in, a guest account tied to a cookie in your browser.

What you write. Your todos and their order, the updates in each thread, files you attach, scheduled todos and reminders.

Your settings. Your time zone, and whether meetings show in your list.

Calendar data, if you connect a calendar. The names of the calendars in each account you connect. For the ones you switch on, the title, times, meeting link and ID of events from yesterday to two weeks ahead. A refresh token, encrypted, so Miller can keep reading them.

What Miller does with it

Only what the product needs: showing your list, your threads and today's meetings to you, and sending the reminders you set. Your data isn't sold, used for advertising, or shown to anyone else. Miller runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

Google and Microsoft calendars

Miller reads your calendar only to list your meetings among your todos. It asks for permission to change events so that moving or adding meetings can come later without a reconnect; it doesn't change your events today.

Miller's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is never used to train AI models, never sold, and never shared except as needed to run Miller.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, or remembers your guest list. One protects forms. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Where it's kept

On Miller's own servers, sent over HTTPS. Calendar refresh tokens are encrypted at rest.

Removing it

A calendar. Settings, then Disconnect. Its meetings leave your list, Miller stops reading it, and its token is deleted (for Google, also revoked). You can also remove Miller from your Google or Microsoft account settings at any time.

A guest list. Guest accounts that never added a todo are deleted after a week.

Your account. Email hello@miller.so from the address you sign in with. Everything is deleted within 30 days.

Changes and contact

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date below changes and signed-in users are told in the app. Questions go to hello@miller.so.
