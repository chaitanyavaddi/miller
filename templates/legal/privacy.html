What Miller collects

Your account. When you sign in with Google: your email address, name and profile picture. Before you sign in, a guest account tied to a cookie in your browser.

What you write. Your todos and their order, the updates in each thread, scheduled todos, and your settings, such as your time zone.

Your calendar, if you connect one. From each connected account's primary Google Calendar: each event's title, start and end time, meeting link, link to the event, ID, status and your response, for events from yesterday to two weeks ahead.

Voice notes, if you use the mic. The audio of what you say into a todo's composer, for up to a minute. It's turned into text for you to edit and send. Miller doesn't keep the audio.

Call recordings, if you start one. While a meeting is on and you choose to record it: your microphone, and the call's audio if you share its tab. The audio is kept only until it's transcribed, then deleted. The transcript is kept with the meeting.

How Miller uses it

Only to provide Miller to you: showing your list, your threads and today's meetings, turning your voice notes into text, and transcribing the calls you record. Your data isn't sold, used for advertising, or shown to anyone else. Miller runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

Google user data

What Miller accesses. With your permission, Miller reads events from your primary Google Calendar using the read-only scope calendar.events.readonly. It never creates, changes or deletes your events. At sign-in it receives your email address, name and profile picture.

How it's used. Only to list your meetings among your todos: the meeting's name and time, a link to join it, a link to open it in Google Calendar, and its ID so that changes and cancellations update the right meeting. Miller hides cancelled events and events you declined.

Who it's shared with. No one. Google user data stays in Miller. It isn't sold, isn't shared with or transferred to third parties, and isn't sent to the transcription service described below.

How it's protected. It's sent over HTTPS. The token that lets Miller read your calendar is encrypted at rest, and only you can see your meetings in Miller.

How long it's kept. Upcoming events are kept up to date as your calendar changes. Meetings you've had stay in your list of past todos until you disconnect the calendar or delete your account. Disconnecting revokes Miller's access and deletes those meetings from Miller.

Limited Use. Miller's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, never sold to data brokers or anyone else, and never used to decide credit or lending. Google Workspace APIs are not used to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI and/or ML models.

Who else processes data

Google, for signing in and, if you connect it, reading your calendar.

Groq, to turn audio into text. Voice notes and call recordings you make are sent to Groq for transcription, and nothing else. No Google user data is sent to Groq.

Recording other people

A call is recorded only when you start it, and only while its meeting is on. Recording a conversation can require everyone's consent where you or they are. Tell the people on the call before you record.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, or remembers your guest list. One protects forms. A draft you haven't sent is kept in your browser's own storage. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Where it's kept, and for how long

On Miller's own servers, sent over HTTPS. Miller keeps your data for as long as you use it. Audio is deleted once it's transcribed. Guest accounts that never added a todo are deleted after a week.

Removing it

A calendar. Settings, then Calendars, then ✕. Its meetings leave Miller, Miller stops reading it, and its access is revoked. You can also remove Miller from your Google Account's third-party access at any time.

Your account. Email hello@miller.so from the address you sign in with. Everything is deleted within 30 days.

Changes and contact

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date below changes and signed-in users are told in the app. Questions go to hello@miller.so.
